# 3.1.0

### SF:music.note Audio Tunes
Tune in to the melody. Audio tunes are now available for the SDA Hymnal to help you sing along.

### SF:pin.fill Pin Hymnals
Keep your favorite hymnals within reach. Pin frequently used books to the top of your list for quicker access.

### SF:magnifyingglass Dedicated Search Tab
A new dedicated Search tab helps you find hymns across both your active hymnal and all pinned books at once.

### SF:square.and.arrow.up Share Hymn Text
Spread the inspiration. You can now easily share the full text of any hymn directly from the share action.
